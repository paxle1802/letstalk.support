Strengthening Minds and Creating Lasting Legacies

An afterschool room in Springhill

Citizens About Progress is a nonprofit in Springhill, Louisiana. We work with 25 young people, from elementary school through high school, on the things that decide how the next ten years go: schoolwork, money, and knowing what is out there.

Program

What happens here

  1. Afterschool tutoring

    Literacy and core subjects.

  2. Homework assistance

    Including group projects.

  3. Financial literacy

    Managing a budget, and what a credit score is and does.

  4. Life skills and character education

    The habits that hold a job and a plan together.

  5. Career exploration

    Field trips and college tours, to widen what a young person knows is possible.

  6. Community and recreation

    Community events and social-recreational activities.

About

About us

Citizens About Progress was recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in June 2020.

The program grew out of something its president kept seeing: young people in schools and colleges carrying debt and damaged credit from spending habits nobody had taught them to question, and losing jobs over work habits nobody had taught them either. Both are learnable. That is the premise the afterschool room is built on.
