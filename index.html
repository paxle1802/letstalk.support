Let’s Talk

A community resource in Fayetteville, North Carolina that promotes physical, social and emotional support. We work to strengthen communities by minimizing barriers to opportunities and equalizing access to resources.

Who we are

A bit of background

Here at Let’s Talk, we are driven by a single goal: to do our part in making the world a better place for all.

Let’s Talk was recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in 2020, and has been part of this community since.

Vision & mission

What we are for

Our vision is to create platforms where people and issues can be heard.

Our mission is to strengthen communities by minimizing barriers to opportunities and equalizing access to resources.

Our ultimate mission is to leave people better than we found them.
